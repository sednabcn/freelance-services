## ❓ Pricing FAQ

**Q: Do you charge hourly or fixed-price?**  
A: Fixed-price for defined projects. Hourly ($150/hour) for consulting or open-ended work.

**Q: What if the scope changes mid-project?**  
A: Small changes are included. Major scope changes require a change order with adjusted pricing.

**Q: Do you offer refunds?**  
A: Yes, if first milestone doesn't meet expectations, full refund of that milestone payment.

**Q: Can I hire you full-time?**  
A: I prefer project-based work, but open to discussing full-time contracts for the right opportunity.

**Q: What currency do you accept?**  
A: USD is standard, but flexible with EUR, GBP, and major cryptocurrencies.
