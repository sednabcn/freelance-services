## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Is web scraping legal?**  
A: Yes, when done ethically. I only scrape publicly available data and respect robots.txt. I never scrape behind authentication without permission.

**Q: What if a website changes?**  
A: I build scrapers with flexibility in mind and include 30 days of free maintenance to handle minor changes.

**Q: How often can the scraper run?**  
A: As often as needed! From real-time to daily/weekly. I'll recommend the best schedule based on your needs.

**Q: What about CAPTCHAs?**  
A: I integrate with CAPTCHA solving services (2Captcha, Anti-Captcha) for legitimate use cases.

**Q: Can you scrape data that requires login?**  
A: Only with explicit permission from the website owner and proper authorization.
